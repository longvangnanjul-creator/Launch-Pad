# Market Analysis Framework

**Product Type:** AI-powered market research and opportunity discovery platform  
**Primary Users:** Entrepreneurs, aspiring entrepreneurs, freelancers, students, professionals, and business owners  
**Core Purpose:** Help users discover market opportunities, understand customer problems, identify demand and competition, and make informed business decisions.

---

## 1. Product Vision

The Market Analysis Framework is an AI-powered platform that helps people understand **what the market wants, what people are struggling with, what businesses are already offering, what is missing, and where potential opportunities exist.**

Instead of giving users a generic market report, the platform guides them through a structured process of questions and research.

The system should turn:

> **Idea → Questions → Market Research → Analysis → Opportunity → Action Plan**

---

## 2. The Problem

People often start businesses based on assumptions such as:

* "I think people will buy this."
* "This skill is probably in demand."
* "Nobody is solving this problem."
* "There aren't many competitors."
* "This product should make money."

The problem is that these assumptions may not reflect the actual market.

Users need a system that helps them understand:

* What people actually need
* What problems people are willing to pay to solve
* Which skills are currently valuable
* Which industries are growing
* What competitors are offering
* What customers dislike about existing solutions
* Where market gaps exist
* What opportunities may be worth investigating

---

## 7. AI Question Engine

This is one of the most important parts of the product.

The AI should **not overwhelm the user with 20–30 questions at once.**

Instead:

> Ask one question → analyze answer → ask next relevant question.

Example:

**AI:**

> What type of businesses do you want to help?

User:

> Small businesses.

AI:

> What problem do small businesses currently have when trying to build websites?

User:

> They don't know who to trust.

AI:

> What makes you believe this is a significant problem?

The system continuously adapts its questions based on previous answers.

## 8. Market Analysis Engine

Once sufficient information has been collected, the platform analyzes the market.

The analysis should contain:

### A. Market Overview

* Industry
* Market category
* Target market
* Geographic market
* Customer segment
* Industry characteristics

### B. Customer Analysis

Identify:

* Who the customer is
* Their needs
* Their problems
* Their frustrations
* Their current alternatives
* Their purchasing behavior
* What they may be willing to pay for

---

## 9. Problem Analysis

The system should identify:

### Customer Problems

What problems exist?

### Problem Frequency

How frequently does the customer experience the problem?

### Problem Severity

How important is the problem?

### Existing Solutions

How are customers currently solving it?

### Solution Weaknesses

What do customers dislike about current solutions?

### Opportunity

Could a better solution potentially address the weakness?

---

## 10. Demand Analysis

The system should investigate indicators such as:

* Search interest
* Customer discussions
* Industry growth
* Product/service demand
* Job postings
* Consumer behavior
* Social-media discussions
* Existing purchases
* Competitor activity

The system should distinguish between:

**Evidence**

and

**AI interpretation.**

This prevents the platform from presenting assumptions as facts.

---

## 11. Competitor Analysis

The platform should identify relevant competitors and organize them into:

| Competitor | Product/Service | Target Customer | Pricing | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

The AI should also investigate:

* What competitors offer
* Their positioning
* Their customer segments
* Their pricing
* Their marketing strategy
* Customer complaints
* Reviews
* Unserved needs

---

## 12. Market Gap Engine

This is a major differentiating feature.

The system should search for gaps such as:

### Product Gap

Customers want something that existing products don't provide.

### Service Gap

Customers struggle to find reliable providers.

### Price Gap

Existing solutions are too expensive for a particular segment.

### Convenience Gap

Existing solutions are complicated or inconvenient.

### Quality Gap

Customers are dissatisfied with quality.

### Accessibility Gap

A solution exists but isn't accessible to a particular customer group.

### Geographic Gap

Demand exists in a location where supply is limited.

### Experience Gap

The product exists but the customer experience is poor.

---

## 13. Opportunity Discovery

After identifying gaps, the AI generates potential opportunities.

Example:

```
CUSTOMER PROBLEM
       ↓
EXISTING SOLUTIONS
       ↓
CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS
       ↓
UNMET NEED
       ↓
MARKET GAP
       ↓
BUSINESS OPPORTUNITY
```

Each opportunity should contain:

* Opportunity name
* Customer
* Problem
* Proposed solution
* Why the opportunity exists
* Existing alternatives
* Potential differentiation
* Required skills
* Potential business model
* Risks
* Validation steps

---

## 14. Skill Demand Analysis

Because the original framework also focuses on **what skills are actually valuable in the market**, this should be a dedicated module.

The system should analyze:

* High-demand skills
* Emerging skills
* Declining/commoditized skills
* Skills associated with business opportunities
* Skills frequently requested by employers
* Skills requested by customers
* Skills that can be combined to create a competitive service

Example:

Market Problem:  
Businesses struggle to automate repetitive tasks.

Relevant skills:

* AI automation
* n8n
* API integration
* CRM automation
* AI agent development

The system should then explain **why each skill is relevant to the identified market problem.**

---

## 15. Opportunity Validation

Before presenting an opportunity as something worth pursuing, the system should ask:

### Demand

Is there evidence that people want this?

### Problem

Is the problem significant?

### Existing Solutions

Are people already paying for alternatives?

### Competition

How crowded is the market?

### Differentiation

Can the user realistically offer something different?

### Resources

Does the user have or can they acquire the required skills/resources?

### Monetization

Are there realistic ways to make money from the opportunity?

---

## 16. Opportunity Scorecard

Instead of simply telling the user "this is the best opportunity," the platform should provide a transparent breakdown.

For example:

| Factor | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Customer demand | Evidence found |
| Problem severity | High/Medium/Low |
| Competition | High/Medium/Low |
| Market gap | Evidence found |
| Monetization | Possible models identified |
| Required skills | Skills identified |
| Main risks | Risks identified |
| Evidence strength | Strong/Moderate/Limited |

This allows the user to make the final decision.

---

## 17. Final Market Report

At the end of the process, the user receives a structured report.

### Report Structure

**1. Executive Summary**

What the research discovered.

**2. Market**

What market is being analyzed.

**3. Target Customer**

Who the customer is.

**4. Customer Problems**

Major problems discovered.

**5. Demand**

Evidence of demand.

**6. Competitors**

Existing solutions and competitors.

**7. Market Gaps**

Potential underserved areas.

**8. Opportunities**

Potential business opportunities.

**9. Required Skills**

Skills relevant to the opportunity.

**10. Business Models**

Possible ways to monetize.

**11. Risks**

Major uncertainties and risks.

**12. Validation Plan**

What the user should test next.

**13. Action Plan**

Concrete next steps.

## 33. Important Product Principle

The platform should **not pretend that AI can guarantee that a business will succeed.**

Instead, it should clearly separate:

**Market Evidence**

from

**AI Analysis**

from

**Potential Opportunity**

from

**User Decision**

The system's job is to improve the user's understanding and decision-making—not make the business decision for them.

---

## 34. One-Sentence Product Definition

> **An AI-powered market intelligence platform that guides users through a structured research process to discover customer problems, analyze demand and competition, identify market gaps, uncover potential opportunities, and turn market research into an actionable business plan.**
